# Avian Visitors v1.3.1

Avian Visitors v1.3.1 adds Birds of America to the [bundle catalog](https://avianvisitors.com/bundles), with more illustration choices for the station and BirdFrame.

![The Avian Visitors Collage using the Birds of America bird bundle](https://github.com/Twarner491/AvianVisitors/releases/download/v1.3.1/birds-of-america-station.jpg)

*The Collage using Birds of America, an optional 439-species set for North America.*

## Birds of America

The new official bundle includes 439 species and 819 illustrations from John James Audubon's Birds of America. Flowers, branches and complete compositions from the original plates bring their natural-history character into the Collage.

Open a bird's postcard to choose among its available illustrations. Your choice is remembered in that browser.

## More illustration choices

The station and BirdFrame now support bundles with multiple illustrations per species. Existing bundles and downloaded artwork continue to work as before.

With a saved location, the device downloads the birds expected in your area and all their illustration choices. Without a location, it downloads the full set.

## Updating an existing station

Use **Tools > Pull latest** or the [documented updater](https://github.com/Twarner491/AvianVisitors/blob/v1.3.1/README.md#updating-an-existing-station). Your current bird bundle stays selected during the update.

After updating, open **Settings > Bird bundle** and choose **Birds of America**, or run the same command on a station or BirdFrame:

```bash
sudo avian-bundle use 'official-birds-of-america'
```
